The Riptide Technology Refresh


In the August of 2024, I was brought on as a outside contractor to refresh the website of a company based out of Washington D.C.
Riptide Technology,

Before our initial meeting they asked me to look over the website and find bugs, visual issues, or other ways to update the site and make it more appealing to potential clients or new employees.

Some of my findings included:
Non-responsive sizing practices
Low resolution assets
Menus and plugins simply not working.

Interactive Sample Below (Linked to the actual site)


And so...The Project Began.

During the initial meeting we agreed to a setup where the site would be fixed in stages.

I would also be provided with a slot in the company's weekly meeting to provide updates on progress and ask questions as needed to ensure good communication and smooth progress.

STAGE 01:
Fix the navigation menu

This stage was where alot of the aforementioned responsive design was needed. They originally only had the large version of their company logo showing regardless of the client screen size, and by using fixed sizing for all links and images, the elements on the site would end up overlapping if the resolution of the screen was too small.

STAGE 05:
Finishing Touches

At this point the original website had been 90% refreshed.

This stage was more about generating new pages over refreshing existing ones.

This would include pages with information about the services the company offered, some light history about the company in a "About Us" page, and a slight refresh of the Contact form by connecting it to a new email and form service.

The new "Services" page would expand on the section on the home page of the same topic, utilizing a simple two column layout to ensure legibility and a simple responsive setup for the information that would collapse to a single column that preserves the important information when viewed on mobile devices.





They also asked that I go through the pages and improve the Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
